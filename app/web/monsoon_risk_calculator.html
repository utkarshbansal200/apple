Mumbai Monsoon Risk Calculator

CE725 · Monte Carlo cost & schedule contingency for monsoon rainfall. Web port of the Streamlit production model (historical-year rainfall replay, 2015–2025). Runs entirely in your browser.

Project settings

Activities

Durations must be whole days. Predecessors separated by “;”. delay_M and cost_M drive rain sensitivity and cost per lost day.

Assumptions and limits

Stop probability per day = min(1, rain/64.5 × sensitivity × multiplier); sensitivity = min(1, delay_M / (0.35 × duration)). The 64.5 mm and 0.35 constants and the one-lost-day-per-stoppage rule are assumed, not fitted to site data. Eleven years of rainfall is a short record for P90. Percentiles are simulated outcomes under these assumptions, not forecasts. Rainfall is the only risk modelled. This web port uses a different random generator from Python/NumPy, so results match the Streamlit tool statistically (within sampling noise), not digit for digit.
